Why it matters

A shop's signbox is its face after dark, and decorative lighting turns a house into a home. Both demand the same fundamentals — correct load wiring, safe drivers and weather-aware fitting — finished with an eye for how it looks, not just how it works.

SCOPE OF WORK

What was done

  • Built and wired illuminated signboxes for shopfronts — framing, lighting layout and power connections.
  • Installed decorative home lighting, planned around rooms and exteriors for warm, even effect.
  • Finished every fitting safely — insulated joints, protected drivers and tidy concealed wiring.
