Why it matters

A home's wiring is buried in its walls for decades — there is no second chance to get it right. This project covers the complete chain: supply intake, protection, distribution and every outlet, each verified by test before handover.

SCOPE OF WORK

What was done

  • Designed and executed a complete single & three-phase electrical wiring layout for residential building applications.
  • Installed service main connections, Main Switch Boards (MSB), sub-distribution panels, circuit breakers (MCB & RCCB) and energy meters.
  • Laid out conduit pathways, pulled concealed/surface wiring, and mounted switches, sockets, light fixtures, and ceiling fans according to standard electrical codes.
  • Performed essential safety and system integrity tests, including insulation resistance tests, continuity checks, and earthing/grounding verification.
