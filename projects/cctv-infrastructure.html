CCTV Surveillance
Infrastructure Setup

End-to-end campus security monitoring for ITCare24BD — structured cabling, PoE switching, centralized storage and remote viewing for the administration.

Project Snapshot

CLIENTITCare24BD

Why it matters

A campus without eyes is a campus without answers. This deployment gives ITCare24BD's administration a single pane of glass over the whole premises — every corridor and gate recorded centrally and viewable remotely.

SCOPE OF WORK

What was done

  • Designed and deployed an end-to-end CCTV camera network for campus security monitoring.
  • Ran and organized structural cabling (Cat6/Coaxial), configured Power over Ethernet (PoE) switches, and mounted strategic indoor/outdoor cameras.
  • Set up centralized NVR/DVR storage, configured IP addressing, and calibrated remote monitoring for institutional administration.
