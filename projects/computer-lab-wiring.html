Computer Lab
Electrical Wiring

Clean, stable power for a multi-station computer laboratory — engineered distribution, proper grounding and balanced phases protecting servers, PCs and UPS backups.

Why it matters

Computers die quietly from bad power — voltage fluctuation, floating grounds and overloaded phases. This project treats the computer lab as what it is: a high-density electrical load that needs the same engineering discipline as any industrial installation.

SCOPE OF WORK

What was done

  • Engineered the electrical distribution layout to power a multi-station computer laboratory efficiently.
  • Installed sub-distribution boards, circuit breakers, trunking, and grounding systems to prevent voltage fluctuation damage.
  • Distributed electrical loads across phases to safely support high-density computing hardware, servers, and UPS backups.
