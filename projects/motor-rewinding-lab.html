Why this lab exists

Motor rewinding is a craft learned by hand — measuring, winding, insulating and testing. Before this facility, students had no dedicated space for it. The lab takes a motor from faulty to factory-healthy in one room: dismantle, rewind, insulate, test, verify.

SCOPE OF WORK

What was done

  • Designed and configured the practical layout for a specialized motor rewinding facility — workflow arranged from strip-down benches to testing stations.
  • Arranged and calibrated rewinding benches, manual & automatic coil winding machines, insulation stripping tools, and testing apparatuses.
  • Prepared testing stations (insulation resistance meters, multimeters) to verify post-rewinding motor health and stator performance.

Result

🔁 From faulty to factory-healthy

Every rewind ends with proof: insulation resistance and continuity readings confirm the stator is healthy before the motor leaves the bench. Students graduate having completed the full cycle themselves — a skill directly employable in workshops across Chattogram and beyond.
