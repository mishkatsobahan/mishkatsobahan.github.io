Why this lab exists

Electrical Technology students need more than textbook diagrams — they need to wire, test and troubleshoot the same control gear used in factories. This lab gives every batch a safe, repeatable environment to practice motor control circuits from the first push-button to a fully protected starter panel.

SCOPE OF WORK

What was done

  • Installed and wired key industrial control components, including magnetic contactors, relays, thermal overload switches, push buttons, and circuit breakers.
  • Implemented standard safety protocols, emergency stop mechanisms, and clearly labelled power/control sections on every board.
  • Built modular circuit boards so instructors can reconfigure experiments quickly and students can practice without disturbing permanent wiring.
